---
name: sage-intake
description: Turn a raw software idea into a scoped SAGE intake, clarification set, risk signal and authority boundary.
---

Use this skill for a new or underspecified software request. Preserve the user's goal and non-goals, identify blocking unknowns, classify preliminary risk, and stop for clarification when unknowns can change scope, authority or safety. Do not choose implementation tools before the contract is clear.
