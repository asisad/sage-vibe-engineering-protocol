---
name: sage-discover
description: Discover and route provider, tool and agent capabilities for an approved SAGE task without granting authority.
---

Use after intake and before planning or implementation when a task needs external capabilities. Translate requirements into capability queries, inspect the local registry, rank the least-side-effect match, and record provenance. Discovery is advisory: it never authorizes execution, expands scope, or bypasses review and approval gates.

When the optional technology-engineering profile applies, discover specialist
profiles by the decision's required capabilities. These are declared on-demand
workflows, not installed toolchains or instantiated agents. An unknown capability
must remain NO_MATCH; do not substitute a familiar language without a new rationale.
