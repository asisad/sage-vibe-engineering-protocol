---
name: sage-plan
description: Create a SAGE evidence-backed implementation plan and provider-neutral architecture delta from an approved intake.
---

Use after intake is clear. Produce the minimum relevant architecture views, preserve stable IDs and boundaries, route only capabilities justified by the task, and define verification, rollback and evidence before implementation. Never treat a generated diagram as the source of truth.

For a new project, material technology choice or added language, use the
optional `sage.technology-engineering` extension when selected by the task.
Record alternatives and versioned evidence against the component's stable ID;
require its technology decision review before implementation. Preserve a suitable
existing stack. Do not impose this comparison on routine changes within an
accepted stack, and do not treat declared specialist profiles as running agents.
